# Product brief

## User and outcome
Describe the intended user, current problem and measurable first value.

## First journey
Trigger, input, steps, output, unhappy path and acceptance evidence.

## Scope and exclusions
Record only agreed work. Label assumptions and delegated decisions.

## Domain and access
Entities, invariants, ownership/roles, sensitive data and retention needs.

## Surfaces and integrations
Public/private/API/worker, external accounts, constraints and missing credentials by name only.

## Agreed decisions
Value, rationale, provenance and date. Do not repeat approved stack questions.
