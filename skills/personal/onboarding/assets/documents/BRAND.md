# Brand foundation

## Context and positioning
Audience, category, desired perception, differentiated promise, proof points, values, personality and explicit refusals.

## Competitive landscape
Dated competitor URLs; observed positioning, messaging, visual patterns, product patterns, category clichés and open opportunities. Separate observation from inference.

## Naming
Strategic territories, candidate shortlist, evaluation, rejected names and reasons, current search/domain/trademark screening, approved name and decision provenance. Screening is not legal clearance.

## Verbal identity
Value proposition, tagline, message hierarchy, voice traits, vocabulary, example copy and brand don'ts.

## Visual identity brief
Logo directions, typography character, palette roles, imagery, iconography, motion and application principles. Link the approved exploration evidence.

## Brand book rules and assets
Logo variants and clear space, minimum sizes, color/type usage, light/dark/monochrome behavior, favicon/OG/social assets, file paths, licensing and ownership notes.
