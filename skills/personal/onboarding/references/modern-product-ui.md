# Modern and future-facing product UI

Apply this to `product_brand` interfaces. The goal is calm capability: simpler to understand and operate, without hiding consequential system behavior.

## Product principles

- Organize the experience around the user's outcome and next meaningful action, not around the system's modules or database entities.
- Give each screen one clear primary action. Use progressive disclosure for secondary controls and advanced paths.
- Use AI where it removes work: interpret, summarize, prefill, transform, recommend, search or execute a bounded workflow. Do not add a chat box when a direct control or structured result is clearer.
- Make AI and asynchronous work observable: show what is happening, useful progress, inputs or sources when relevant, completion, failure and the next available action.
- Preserve control for consequential actions: preview or confirm when warranted, allow cancel/undo/retry, keep history, and make generated output editable.
- Keep navigation and spatial structure stable even when content adapts. Personalization must not make controls unpredictable.
- Prefer concise copy, strong information hierarchy, direct manipulation, useful visuals and context-aware actions over dense toolbars, permanent side panels and repeated explanatory text.
- Keep expert depth available without placing it in every user's default path.

## What to avoid

- dashboard sprawl copied from legacy CRM products;
- generic AI landing pages assembled from gradient heroes, identical bento grids and vague claims;
- conversational UI for every task;
- hiding critical state or permissions behind apparent simplicity;
- excessive empty space that lowers information value;
- autonomous actions without boundaries, evidence or recovery.

Modern does not mean visually empty. Use the density required for the decision, but make hierarchy, grouping and action priority immediately legible. Research current direct competitors and emerging interaction patterns for each serious product; do not freeze one 2026 aesthetic as a permanent house style.
