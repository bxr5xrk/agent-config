# Choose onboarding depth first

Select the track before deep discovery. The track controls process depth, not implementation quality.

## Lightweight site

Use `lightweight_site` when the work is primarily a small informational or utility website and most of these are true:

- content and purpose are already known;
- there is no substantial authenticated product journey;
- a new defensible brand or market category is not part of the goal;
- the cost of choosing an ordinary professional direction is low and reversible;
- the user values speed and clarity over strategic differentiation.

Typical examples: a personal page, event page, simple brochure site, temporary campaign page, internal utility or a small site under an existing identity.

Output: a short brief, one coherent design direction chosen with normal Codex judgment, compact tokens, the required page/components and proportionate verification. Skip branding specialists, broad competitor research, naming exercises, logo exploration, six-option boards and brand-book work unless explicitly requested.

## Product and brand

Use `product_brand` when any material signal applies:

- the user says SaaS, B2B software, product, startup, platform, commercial application or new brand;
- naming, trust, category positioning or differentiation can affect adoption;
- there is an ongoing authenticated workflow, repeated use, multiple roles or meaningful product state;
- the interface is intended to become a durable product rather than one disposable page;
- the user explicitly asks for brand, competitor, naming, logo, design-system or multiple-direction work.

Output: the full brief, competitor evidence, positioning, naming checks, visual identity, BRAND.md, 5–10 rendered directions, selected design system and verified first journey.

## Routing rule

Infer the track from explicit language. Do not ask whether an explicitly described SaaS/B2B product is a serious product, and do not interrogate a clearly small site. When the evidence is mixed and the choice changes the deliverables, ask one question with `lightweight_site` and `product_brand` as the meaningful options and explain the recommendation. Record a short concrete reason, not just the label.

Reclassify when new facts warrant it. A minimal-looking interface can still be a serious product; visual simplicity is not evidence that branding and product reasoning are unnecessary.
