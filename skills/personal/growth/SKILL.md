# Growth

Turn a growth question into a measurable business loop, not a list of tactics.
Work directly when invoked; a build orchestrator is optional.

Read the [shared project context](../build/references/context.md), the product's
current positioning and funnel evidence, and the actual channel/product
constraints. Start with a narrow ICP, painful job, buying trigger, and reachable
distribution surface. Map the requested work across acquisition, activation,
retention, expansion, and revenue, but optimize only the bottleneck supported by
evidence. Do not disguise product or demand uncertainty with more outreach.

Define a falsifiable hypothesis, observable event path, primary metric, guardrail,
time/cost bound, and stop condition before recommending an experiment. Separate
leading activity from business outcomes. For early B2B products, paid-pilot or
equivalent commitment evidence is stronger than compliments, waitlists, or
unqualified traffic.

Read only the applicable references:

- ICP, problem, journey, loop selection, activation and retention:
  [growth model](references/growth-model.md).
- Experiment design, instrumentation, attribution and decision rules:
  [experiments and measurement](references/experiments-measurement.md).
- Cold outbound, social/platform-dependent acquisition, first-message tooling,
  deliverability and channel risk: [acquisition channels](references/acquisition-channels.md).
- Pricing, unit economics, pilots, validation gates, and competitor categories:
  [economics and validation](references/economics-validation.md).

Growth recommendations do not authorize messaging prospects, buying ads,
changing prices, or violating platform terms. Confirm authority before external
execution. Avoid dark patterns, deceptive personalization, spam, scraped sensitive
data, or tactics whose expected value depends on account abuse. State gray-market
or platform-enforcement risk as a core business constraint, not a footnote.

Update canonical positioning, analytics, or experiment documents only when the
request authorizes it. Finish via the [closeout contract](../build/references/closeout.md):
standalone growth work audits its assumptions and measurement path; delegated
work returns hypotheses, evidence, economics, risks, and decision gates. Apply
[automatic feedback learning](../build/references/feedback.md) without converting
one project's channel choice into universal policy.
