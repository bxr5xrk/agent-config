# Acquisition channels and platform risk

Evaluate a channel by ICP reach, intent, cost, speed, controllability, saturation,
measurement quality, and dependency risk. Separate owned distribution from paid,
partner, community, search, marketplace, and outbound channels.

For cold first-message outreach, compare tools that can actually initiate the
first contact on the target channel. Responders, comment-to-DM automations, inbox
CRMs, lead databases, and managed agencies may be adjacent but are not substitutes.
Verify current capability, account requirements, message controls, and pricing.

Model platform-dependent constraints explicitly:

- official API/terms versus private API, browser automation, or manual operation;
- account/session/proxy dependence and identity separation;
- quotas, deliverability, warm-up, review, blocks, and appeal paths;
- recipient consent, privacy, jurisdiction, and brand/reputation impact;
- provider churn, detection changes, maintenance load, and gross-margin cost;
- safe stop conditions and blast-radius containment.

Do not recommend evasion or account-abuse mechanics. If the business relies on a
gray-market channel, name that dependency, assess whether customers accept it,
and require an alternative distribution path or bounded exposure before treating
it as scalable.

Judge outbound on qualified conversations, meetings, paid pilots, retained
revenue, complaints, and account health—not volume sent. Personalization must be
truthful and based on appropriately obtained data.
