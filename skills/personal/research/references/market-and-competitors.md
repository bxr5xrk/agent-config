# Market and competitor research

Define the category by the job actually performed before selecting competitors.
Products that respond after a lead messages, send true cold first messages,
discover prospects, manage inboxes, or provide agency services are adjacent but
not interchangeable. Compare only on the capability relevant to the decision and
call out category mismatches.

For each relevant competitor verify the current product, target customer,
mechanism, channel/platform dependency, pricing or sales motion, and evidence of
traction. Prefer first-party pricing, documentation, terms, product UI, release
notes, and customer proof; use independent sources for market outcomes and
failure modes. Date unstable facts. Do not infer scale from follower counts,
template directories, or marketplace presence alone.

For opportunity research connect evidence to a specific ICP and painful job:

- who owns the problem and budget;
- current workaround and switching trigger;
- required data or distribution advantage;
- platform, regulatory, operational, and gross-margin constraints;
- credible wedge and why incumbents do not trivially copy it;
- the cheapest evidence that would disprove demand.

Recommendations should state the decision criteria and why the selected option
wins them, not produce a generic top-ten list. When substantial time or money is
at stake, verify current availability and include the material downside.

Do not smuggle project-specific facts into global conclusions. A concrete
repository may supply a case study; generalize only the supported mechanism or
decision rule.
