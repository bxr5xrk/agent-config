# Research deliverables

Match depth of investigation to consequence, then match the output to the user's
requested size. Deep work may legitimately produce a one-sentence answer.

## Exact short answer

Lead with the mechanism or conclusion, not the research process. Include the
critical source/location qualifier inside the sentence when it prevents a wrong
interpretation. Do not append a report, caveats list, or next steps unless the
question requires them.

## Comparison or decision memo

State the decision first, then the few criteria that change it. Use a compact
table only for repeated exact fields. Keep dated evidence and uncertainty beside
the affected item. Distinguish “not verified” from “not available”.

## Technical investigation

Give the observed behavior, the traced path, and the material condition or gap.
Use clickable file/line references. For diagnosis, separate cause from symptom
and reproduction from inference.

## Research dossier

Organize around the decision: thesis, evidence, counterevidence, alternatives,
risks, and recommendation. Attach a claim ledger or source appendix only when it
improves auditability. Avoid padding with methodology, source inventories, or
background the user did not request.

If an item cannot be established, say exactly which claim remains open and why;
still complete all independent parts.
